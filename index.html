OUR JOURNEY

The Story Behind SattaCraft

What started as FrameBook during my college days was more than just a design experiment. It was the beginning of a journey filled with creativity, learning, and countless hours of improving my craft.

As experience grew, so did the vision. FrameBook evolved into SattaCraft — inspired by the Tamil word "Sattagam" (Frame) and combined with "Craft" to represent artistic excellence and thoughtful design.

Today, SattaCraft creates custom photo frames, artistic wall posters, and personalized artworks designed to transform memories into meaningful pieces of art.
